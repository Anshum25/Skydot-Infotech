What is the difference between Frappe and ERPNext?
Frappe is the open-source web framework on which ERPNext is built. ERPNext is the full ERP application. We work with both — customizing ERPNext for your business or building standalone Frappe apps from scratch.
How long does an ERPNext implementation take?
A standard implementation takes 6 to 14 weeks depending on scope. Simple setups with 2 to 3 modules can go live in 4 weeks. Complex multi-site deployments take 3 to 4 months.
Can you migrate our data from Tally, SAP, or Excel?
Yes. We have migrated data from Tally, SAP B1, SAP HANA, Oracle, Dynamics 365, QuickBooks, and Excel. Data accuracy is non-negotiable for us.
Do you offer post-go-live support?
Yes. All projects include a minimum 3-month post-go-live support period. We also offer annual AMC plans.
Can we hire a dedicated Frappe or ERPNext developer from your team?
Yes. You can hire dedicated developers or consultants on a full-time, part-time, or project basis. Most clients onboard within 48 hours.
